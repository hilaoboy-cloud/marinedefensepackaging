MIL-STD-2073 Reference Guide

Quick reference for military preservation methods and packaging codes:

Method 10 - Physical Protection [M-10]
Unsealed container providing mechanical and physical protection against transit shock and abrasion.
Method 20 - Preservative Coating [M-20]
Application of MIL-PRF-16173 or VCI anti-corrosion barrier oil/film directly to metal surfaces.
Method 30 - Waterproof Barrier [M-30]
Enclosure inside sealed MIL-DTL-117 waterproof bag with heat-sealed seams.
Method 50 - Water-Vaporproof Desiccant [M-50]
Hermetically sealed barrier bag with active MIL-D-3464 desiccant packets for critical electronics.
