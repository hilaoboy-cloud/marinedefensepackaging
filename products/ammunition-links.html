Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging manufactures high-precision metallic disintegrating ammunition feed links engineered strictly to US Military Specifications: MIL-L-45402 (M13 links), MIL-L-45404 (M27 links), and MIL-L-45403 (M9 links). Designed for flawless cyclic feeding in modern infantry, naval, and airborne automatic weapon systems.

Fabricated from premium cold-rolled high-carbon spring steel, heat-treated in continuous atmosphere-controlled belt furnaces to exact Rockwell hardness criteria, and finished with heavy manganese phosphate coatings per MIL-DTL-16232. Delivering exceptional spring retention, smooth cartridge stripping, and zero jamming at cyclic firing rates exceeding 1,000 rounds per minute.

Zero-Jamming Reliability & Quality Validation

Every link production lot undergoes 100% automated optical multi-sensor dimensional inspection and rigorous pull-force / stripping-force testing. Packaged in moisture-resistant barrier cartons or pre-loaded directly inside M19A1 and M2A1 ammunition cans ready for front-line deployment.

Engineering Features & Ballistic Cycling Standards

Engineered to deliver flawless automatic firing reliability across all climatic combat environments.

SPRING STEEL

High-Carbon Spring Steel (SAE 1050/1065)

Formed on high-speed multi-slide progressive stamping presses from precision cold-rolled spring steel for uniform wall thickness and dimensional consistency across millions of links.

ATMOSPHERIC HEAT TREATMENT

Atmosphere-Controlled Austempering

Continuous mesh belt furnaces with protective atmosphere prevent surface decarburization, producing a fine bainitic/martensitic microstructure with optimum toughness and zero brittleness.

MILITARY PHOSPHATE

MIL-DTL-16232 Manganese Phosphate

Heavy non-reflective manganese phosphate coating absorbs and retains protective lubricants, reducing friction during rapid cyclic bolt stripping and resisting marine corrosion.

OPTICAL SORTING

100% High-Speed Vision Inspection

Automated multi-camera vision systems verify loop diameters, pitch alignment, and burr-free edges at high speeds, to keep defects out of defense inventory.

High-Volume Arsenal Supply & Belted Packaging

Delivering high-rate production quantities directly to government arsenals and commercial ammunition manufacturers.

HIGH OUTPUT

Multi-Million Link Monthly Capacity

Automated progressive tooling cells and multi-station finishing lines produce tens of millions of precision links monthly, supporting major defense stockpiling programs.

PACKAGING OPTIONS

Bulk Cartons or Pre-Loaded Cans

Delivered in bulk vapor-barrier lined cartons, or pre-packed directly inside M19A1, M2A1, and PA108 ammunition containers complete with internal separators and desiccant.

LOT INTEGRITY

Rigid MIL-STD-1916 Lot Control

Every production lot is assigned unique identification numbers with full traceability to raw coil melt certificates, heat-treat recorder logs, and stripping force charts.

RAPID DISPATCH

Allied Stockpile Readiness

Strategic reserve inventories maintained in key logistics hubs allow rapid emergency dispatch to allied armed forces encountering operational ammunition shortages.
