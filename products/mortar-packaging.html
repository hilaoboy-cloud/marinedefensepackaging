Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging manufactures military-grade waterproof mortar packaging systems engineered to protect infantry mortar ammunition under all tactical conditions. Built strictly to US Military Specification MIL-DTL-45427, PPP-P-1896, and UK MoD DEF-STAN 81-41.

Our product range encompasses spiral-wound waterproof fiberboard container tubes and heavy-gauge steel hermetic containers (including the PA116 series) designed for 60mm (M224), 81mm (M252), and 120mm (M120/M121) mortar rounds. Featuring multi-ply asphaltic or aluminum foil moisture barrier laminates, heavy crimped steel or polymer end-caps, molded fin-protection base cups, and integrated shock-damping fuze buffers.

Infantry Tactical Protection & Water Submersion

Engineered for combat troop handling and tactical air drops. Tested for complete water-tightness under full submersion and extreme temperatures (-40°C to +71°C), so mortar propellant charges, sensitive primers, and electronic fuzes stay dry and combat-ready.

Engineering Features & Ballistic Protection

Every mortar packaging tube is engineered with specialized internal geometry to lock the round securely along its ballistic axis.

HERMETIC INTEGRITY

Multi-Ply Foil Moisture Barrier

Spiral-wound walls incorporate heavy-duty aluminum foil or asphaltic vapor barrier plies, achieving near-zero moisture vapor transmission rates (MVTR) to protect hygroscopic propellant charges.

FIN INTEGRITY

Molded Fin-Protector Base Cups

High-density polyethylene bottom cups cradle the tail-fin assembly, isolating sensitive ignition cartridges from dynamic transit shocks and preventing fin bending that degrades mortar accuracy.

FUZE BUFFER

Integrated Fuze Cushioning Buffer

Top end-caps feature energy-absorbing closed-cell foam buffers engineered to hold the nose fuze firmly without applying axial stress to delicate mechanical or proximity fuze threads.

RAPID EXTRACTION

Quick-Pull Tear Strips & Cam Lids

Designed for rapid combat employment, tubes feature pull-tab tear tapes or quick-turn twist caps that can be opened in seconds even by soldiers wearing heavy combat gloves.

High-Volume Tube Winding & Arsenal Integration

Modern automated converting lines provide rapid, reliable deliveries for high-volume mortar production contracts.

SPIRAL CONVERTING

Automated Multi-Spindle Tube Winding

Continuous spiral winding machines with inline adhesive metering produce seamless, high-crush-strength tubes with micro-tolerance internal diameters.

STEEL CRIMPING

Heavy Metal End-Cap Seaming

Double-lock seaming machines crimp zinc-plated steel bottom lids securely to the fiber body, reinforced with waterproof elastomeric sealants.

COMPLETE PACK PACKAGING

Outer Wooden Crates

Supplied as complete packaging sets, including the outer hardwood crates configured for 3-round or 2-round tactical packs.

RAPID CONTRACT FULFILLMENT

Allied Stockpile Surge Scaling

Multi-million tube production capacity ensures fast delivery to ammunition arsenals and mortar propellant loading plants worldwide.
