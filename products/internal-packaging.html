Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging engineers and manufactures custom internal munitions protective packaging sets for ammunition containers, tactical transit cases, and artillery crates. Built strictly to US Military Specifications, including MIL-PRF-26514 (polyurethane foam), PPP-C-1797 (polyethylene foam), and MIL-PRF-131K (vapor barrier packaging).

Our internal packaging solutions include custom CNC-routed closed-cell polyethylene foam inserts, multi-cavity thermoformed trays, die-cut fiberboard partitions, and the PA125 25mm injection-molded plastic separator sets and restraining plates for automatic cannon ammunition cans. Engineered to provide complete shock mitigation, vibration isolation, and electrostatic discharge (ESD) protection for delicate fuzes, missile seeker heads, and high-explosive ordnance.

Cleanliness & Chemical Neutrality

Manufactured using military-grade, non-corrosive, non-hydroscopic polymers. Absolutely free of corrosive plasticizers, sulfur compounds, and abrasive particles. Compatible with optical seeker domes, electronic fuzing circuitry, and precision gold/copper tactical connectors.

Engineering Features & Shock Dissipation Standards

Engineered and verified through finite element analysis (FEA) and dynamic drop testing to isolate sensitive ordnance from high-G transit shocks.

PRECISION ROUTING

Multi-Axis CNC Waterjet & Router Cutting

Precision multi-axis abrasive waterjet and CNC router tables carve complex 3D internal cavities directly from 3D CAD models within ±0.5 mm tolerances, locking payloads firmly in place.

ELECTROSTATIC CONTROL

Full ESD Dissipative Shielding

Conductive and static-dissipative foam compounds dissipate triboelectric charges, safeguarding electro-explosive devices (EEDs), thermal batteries, and proximity fuzes from accidental initiation.

NON-CORROSIVE

Non-Offgassing & Chemical Neutrality

Foams are nitrogen-blown without CFCs or toxic chemical blowing agents. Completely non-hydroscopic (zero moisture absorption) and chemically non-reactive to painted steel, brass, and aluminum.

EXTREME RESILIENCE

MIL-STD-810H Shock & Drop Certified

Cushioning geometries tested to absorb high-velocity 5-foot drops without bottoming out, keeping peak deceleration well below critical payload G-limits.

Custom Prototyping, Clean Room Converting & Scaling

Turnkey design, prototyping, and high-volume converting for major military weapon programs.

RAPID CAD/DFM

Rapid Prototype Turnaround

In-house packaging engineers transform defense customer STEP/IGES CAD files into custom drop-testable foam prototypes within 48 to 72 hours.

CLEAN ROOM CONVERTING

Clean, Particle-Free Converting

Converted in controlled environments and cleaned with ionized air blowers to keep sensitive defense optics and electronic fuzes free of particulate contamination.

KIT INTEGRATION

Turnkey Pre-Kitting Services

Internal foam and separator components can be delivered pre-installed directly inside ammunition cans or cases, ready for immediate loading on arsenal filling lines.

HIGH CAPACITY

Automated Converting Capacity

Multiple automated die-cutting and laminating presses easily support high-rate monthly production volumes for high-priority allied weapons programs.
