Overview

Marine Defense Packaging manufactures laminated paperboard filler, separator and protective internal packaging components for ammunition containers.

Our filler sheet is manufactured to meet MIL-PRF-50449C (Filler, Sheet Form — Laminated Paper, for Use in Ammunition Containers) and is tested against its requirements. Components are cut to customer drawings, specifications and technical data packages. Paperboard, fibreboard and other approved materials are also available for non-ammunition applications.

Material Specification

Our laminated paperboard filler is manufactured to meet the requirements of MIL-PRF-50449C — Performance Specification: Filler, Sheet Form (Laminated Paper, for Use in Ammunition Containers) — and is tested against those requirements. The specification defines a solid, water-resistant sheet material used as a filler or cushioning pad inside ammunition containers during transportation and storage.

RequirementMIL-PRF-50449C
MaterialLaminated / coated paperboard meeting all specification requirements; chemically neutral, non-reactive, non-static and non-hazardous; non-metallic; clean, uniform appearance, free of grease, dirt and oil
Thickness0.062 in (+0.010 / -0.008) — supplied at 1.6 mm nominal
Water resistanceMaximum 15% water absorption by weight; no delamination after 48 hours of submersion
Heat stabilityNo delamination, tackiness, oozing, blistering or off-gassing after 160°F for 24 hours minimum
BendingBends in both directions without cracking or delamination
StiffnessDrape not exceeding 40 degrees
ColorDark, non-reflective (black, gray, brown or dark green) or natural kraft
CleanlinessFree of metal inclusions and foreign odors
Recycled contentThe specification encourages the use of recycled, recovered or environmentally preferable materials wherever requirements are met

Cut dimensions are not fixed by the specification — finished sizes, quantities and packaging follow the customer-approved drawing or technical data package for the applicable container.
