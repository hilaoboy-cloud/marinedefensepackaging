Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging designs and manufactures heavy-duty ordnance handling and storage bomb pallets engineered strictly to US Military Specifications, including the MHU-110, MHU-141, and MHU-191/M series and MIL-STD-1323. Built to support aircraft carrier flight decks, forward operating air bases, and naval weapons stations.

Engineered to safely transport, cradle, and stage heavy aircraft ordnance — including Mk 82 (500 lb), Mk 83 (1,000 lb), Mk 84 (2,000 lb) General Purpose Bombs, BLU penetrators, and Joint Direct Attack Munitions (JDAM). Fabricated from heavy-gauge structural carbon steel or high-strength aluminum alloys, featuring precision radius saddle cutouts, non-sparking elastomer liners, and high-load lashing points.

Flight Deck Safety & NAVSEA Shock Certification

Engineered to meet stringent naval flight deck standards, including MIL-STD-1323 shipboard dynamic roll and pitch restraint criteria and MIL-S-901D high-impact shock requirements. Designed to prevent ordnance rolling, chaffing, or shifting during high sea-state maritime operations.

Engineering Features & Flight-Deck Standards

Engineered for safe handling and rapid turnaround during intense air wing launch and recovery cycles.

HEAVY TIE-DOWN

10,000 lb Proof-Load Lashing D-Rings

Equipped with forged high-strength steel D-rings rated to 10,000 lbs proof load. Accommodates standard naval tie-down chains and quick-release ratchet straps to secure payloads in rough sea conditions.

SPARK-FREE SADDLES

Neoprene & EPDM Anti-Spark Liners

Precision-milled ordnance saddles are lined with heavy vulcanized neoprene or conductive anti-static elastomer. Prevents metal-to-metal sparking and dampens harmful transit vibrations.

TACTICAL FORKLIFT

4-Way Rough-Terrain Fork Pockets

Reinforced full-length tubular steel fork pockets allow 4-way entry for tactical rough-terrain forklifts, naval flight-deck weapon loaders, and standard warehouse handling equipment.

MARINE CORROSION

Hot-Dip Galvanized & Epoxy Coating

Coated with heavy hot-dip galvanizing or high-build marine epoxy primers and polyurethane topcoats, providing outstanding salt-spray resistance in harsh maritime environments.

Certified Welding, Structural Testing & Turnkey Supply

Delivering high-reliability ordnance handling hardware built to rigorous naval defense standards.

AWS CERTIFIED WELDING

Robotic & Certified Manual Welds

Fabricated by AWS D1.1 certified welders and robotic welding stations. 100% visual weld inspection and magnetic particle testing (MT) verify complete joint penetration and crack-free durability.

PROOF LOAD TESTING

Static & Dynamic Load Validation

Every prototype and production sampling is tested under 200% static proof loading to verify structural integrity before flight-deck deployment.

MODULAR STACKING

Empty Returnable Stacking Geometry

Designed with interlocking stacking feet and nesting profiles. Empty pallets nest together tightly to save critical space on flight decks and in shipboard ordnance magazines.

GLOBAL PROGRAM DELIVERY

Turnkey Defense Contract Supply

Supplying naval weapon stations, allied air forces, and defense primes with fully documented, ready-to-deploy bomb pallet assemblies complete with full test certifications.
