Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging manufactures high-performance injection-molded tactical transit cases certified to international military standards, including MIL-STD-810H, IP67 / IP68 waterproof ratings, STANAG 4280, and DEF-STAN 81-41.

Engineered to protect high-value mission hardware — including precision sniper weapons, electro-optical night vision sensors, missile guidance components, tactical communications systems, and drone electronics. Constructed from ultra-high-impact structural polypropylene copolymer, featuring continuous neoprene O-ring seals, automatic ambient pressure equalization valves, heavy-duty stainless steel hinge pins, and custom CNC-machined foam interiors.

Extreme Environmental Sealing & Impact Resilience

100% dust-tight and submersible in water up to 1 meter depth. Engineered to withstand high-velocity vehicle drops, sub-zero arctic impacts (-40°C), desert heat (+80°C), and altitude pressure differentials during tactical aircraft combat airdrops.

Engineering Features & Tactical Housing Standards

Engineered for maximum reliability and field convenience under high-stress combat operations.

PRESSURE EQUALIZATION

Automatic Gore-Tex Purge Valve

Micro-porous hydrophobic Teflon membrane equalizes internal atmospheric pressure during rapid altitude changes while preventing water droplets and dust from penetrating the case interior.

SUBMERSIBLE SEAL

Continuous Neoprene O-Ring Gasket

Molded elastomeric tongue-and-groove neoprene gasket maintains continuous compression along the entire lid perimeter, sealing against sand, chemical vapors, and high-pressure water jets.

STRUCTURAL RESIN

High-Impact Polypropylene Copolymer

Proprietary structural resin formulation with mineral reinforcement provides extreme flexural yield strength, absorbing massive kinetic impact shocks without shattering or permanent denting.

TACTICAL HARDWARE

Stainless Steel Pins & Double-Throw Latches

Heavy-duty corrosion-resistant 316 stainless steel hinge pins and smooth double-throw latches ensure reliable opening in cold conditions and prevent accidental unlatching during drop impact.

Custom Foam Machining, Arsenal Branding & Supply

Delivering complete turn-key transit housing assemblies customized to defense customer specifications.

CUSTOM INTERIORS

Precision CNC Waterjet Foam Inserts

Every case can be outfitted with custom multi-layer cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) or conductive ESD foam cushioning, precision routed to match weapon profiles and accessories.

TACTICAL COLORS

Mil-Spec Molded Color Options

Available in standard military colors: Black, Olive Drab Green (FED-STD-595 34079), Desert Tan (33446), and Coyote Brown with molded-in non-reflective matte finish.

ELECTRONIC INTEGRATION

Panel-Mount & Bezel Integration

Interior rim supports custom aluminum or composite bezel mounting rings, allowing direct integration of electronic instrument panels, tactical displays, and field testing equipment.

VOLUME SCALE

Large-Platen Injection Molding

High-tonnage injection molding machines and multi-cavity tooling provide rapid production throughput to fulfill large-scale defense modernization contracts.
