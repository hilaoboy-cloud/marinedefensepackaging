155mm Pallet Systems

Marine Defense Packaging manufactures 155mm ammunition pallet systems for storage, handling and transportation applications.

Our build-to-print approach allows pallet configurations to be manufactured according to customer-controlled drawings, specifications and approved technical requirements.

Different pallet configurations can support different loading arrangements, packaging requirements and logistics applications.

Final pallet configuration is determined by the applicable customer drawing, technical data package and contract requirements.

Pallet Types

TYPE 01

Standard 155mm Pallets

Wooden pallet systems designed for unitizing 155mm ammunition for storage, handling and transportation applications.

TYPE 02

Vertical Pallet Systems

Vertical loading configurations designed to securely retain multiple 155mm ammunition units during handling and transportation.

TYPE 03

Heavy-Duty / Reinforced Pallets

Reinforced pallet constructions for demanding handling, transportation and long-distance logistics applications.

TYPE 04

Custom Configuration Pallets

Customer-specific pallet configurations manufactured to approved drawings, loading arrangements and packaging requirements.

TYPE 05

Knock-Down / Export Configurations

Packaging configurations designed to support storage and shipping efficiency where permitted by the applicable packaging design and program requirements.

Manufactured to Your Drawings

Marine Defense Packaging works from customer-controlled drawings, specifications and approved revisions to manufacture packaging products for defense applications.

Our manufacturing process is focused on consistent production, controlled material sourcing and repeatable assembly according to the applicable technical requirements.

Process

STEP 01

STEP 05

STEP 06
