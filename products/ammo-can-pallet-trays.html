Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging manufactures the NATO-approved M2A1 patent-pending ammunition pallet tray system (built to Drawing 19200-12982864 and MIL-STD-1904). Developed as a highly efficient, cost-effective, and fully reusable alternative to traditional disposable wooden wirebound crates.

The 5-tray system securely palletizes 96 × M2A1 ammunition cans (or 48 × PA108 containers) on a standard 40" × 48" NATO pallet footprint. A complete unit load can be assembled and banded by a single warehouse operative in under 15 minutes. Compatible with any metal ammunition container sharing the M2A1 footprint — including M2A2, H83, PA19, PA60, and PA70.

Logistical Efficiency & Reusability

Eliminates the continuous cost and disposal burden of single-use wooden crates. Trays interlock vertically to create a rigid, unified monolithic pallet block that withstands rail shunting, ocean transit, and rough-terrain field deployment, returning flat for circular reuse.

Engineering Features & NATO Logistics Standards

Engineered to maximize ammunition handling speed, dynamic transit stability, and long-term depot utility.

SPEED DEPLOYMENT

15-Minute Single-Operator Assembly

Simple modular stacking workflow allows a single warehouse worker to fully stage, tray, and band 96 ammunition cans in under 15 minutes, drastically lowering arsenal packing labor overhead.

PRECISION RECESSED POCKETS

CNC Machined Locating Grids

Precision-contoured recesses securely seat the bottom rim and top handle embossing of each ammunition container, locking the entire unit against lateral slippage during rough transport.

EXTREME LOAD RATING

4,000 lb Dynamic Capacity

Engineered from high-impact structural polymers or engineered exterior-grade composite plywood, supporting over 4,000 lbs dynamic transit load and warehouse vertical stacking up to 3 tiers high.

CIRCULAR ECONOMY

Returnable Closed-Loop Life Cycle

Unlike single-use wooden wirebound boxes that are destroyed upon de-packaging, intermediate trays can be nested together and returned flat for dozens of repeated supply loops.

Standardized NATO Intermodal Transport

Delivering turn-key palletizing systems optimized for automated defense supply chains.

COMPATIBLE PALLETS

Standard 40" × 48" NATO Footprint

Fits directly onto standard military wood or steel 40" × 48" pallets, ISO shipping containers, NATO 463L air cargo pallets, and European railway wagons without overhang.

BANDING INTEGRATION

Molded Strapping Retainer Channels

Features integrated strapping guide channels that prevent high-tensile polyester or steel bands from slipping off corners, maintaining full clamping tension throughout intermodal shipping.

WEATHER RESISTANCE

Chemical & Water Resistant Materials

Resistant to battery acid, oils, hydraulic fluids, and prolonged moisture exposure. Does not rot, mold, or support bacterial infestation during open depot staging.

SCALABLE PRODUCTION

Rapid Volume Manufacturing

High-volume production lines ready to supply thousands of tray modules for major national ammunition containerization initiatives.
