Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging supplies mission-critical tactical ammunition containers and metal ammo cans engineered strictly to US Department of Defense Technical Data Packages (TDP) and military specifications, including MIL-DTL-3060G, MIL-C-70628, MIL-C-70842, MIL-C-70998, and MIL-S-50312.

Our portfolio encompasses 18+ in-service military container models actively supporting allied armed forces worldwide. Every container is built from premium cold-rolled carbon steel, featuring automated continuous resistance seam welding, high-durability EPDM synthetic rubber hermetic gaskets, and robotic Chemical Agent Resistant Coating (CARC). Engineered to ensure 100% moisture-tight, airtight, and salt-fog resistant protection for small arms ammunition, medium caliber linked rounds, 40mm grenades, mortar rounds, and precision missile hardware.

Defense Standardization & Hermetic Quality

Every production lot is subjected to 100% submerged airtight leak testing at 1.5 to 2.0 PSI dry air, verifying hermetic seal integrity. Validated under MIL-STD-1916 sampling plans and built to UN POP 4A dangerous goods packaging requirements for Class 1 explosive munitions.

Engineering Features & Military Quality Standards

Engineered to withstand the world's most severe operational environments, including extreme combat temperatures, salt spray, rough-terrain shock, and long-term depot storage.

HERMETIC INTEGRITY

100% Submerged Airtight Leak Testing

Every manufactured container is 100% submerged and pressurized to 1.5–2.0 PSI with dry compressed air. Custom molded EPDM synthetic rubber gaskets provide a hermetic seal against moisture, sand, chemical agents, and water immersion.

STRUCTURAL STEEL

Cold-Rolled Carbon Steel & Seam Welding

Formed from premium AISI 1008/1010 cold-rolled steel sheet with automated continuous seam and projection resistance welding. Eliminates porosity and delivers exceptional structural rigidity under extreme drop impacts.

MILITARY FINISH

CARC Military Powder Coating

Treated with a multi-stage zinc phosphate pre-treatment followed by MIL-DTL-53039 / MIL-DTL-53022 Chemical Agent Resistant Coating (CARC Green 383 or Desert Tan 686). Exceeds 96+ hours ASTM B117 salt spray corrosion resistance.

QUICK DEPLOYMENT

Quick-Release Cam-Action Latch

Heavy-duty stamped spring-steel cam-action latch mechanism provides rapid opening in field combat conditions while maintaining positive clamp tension during dynamic transit. Features integrated wire security seal holes.

LOGISTICS STACKING

Interlocking Stacking Ribs

Precision embossed lid recesses and bottom base ribs provide positive-locking tiered stacking. Prevents shifting on standard 40" × 48" pallets during multi-tier warehouse stacking up to 5 layers high.

QUALITY CERTIFICATION

Mil-STD-1916 & UN POP 4A Certified

Manufactured and verified in accordance with MIL-STD-1916 sampling plans and MIL-STD-406 visual inspection criteria. Full UN POP 4A packaging certification for international transport of Class 1 explosives.

Packaging Logistics, Palletization & Surge Fulfillment

Supplying major defense ministries, NATO armed forces, and allied munitions manufacturers with standardized palletized container deliveries.

NATO PALLETIZATION

Standardized Pallet Tier Configurations

Delivered on heat-treated ISPM 15 wooden pallets or heavy-duty steel intermediate pallet trays. M2A1 containers are packed in standardized 96-can modules (or 48-can PA108 modules) secured with heavy-duty polyester or steel banding.

SURGE MANUFACTURING

High-Speed Stamping & Welding Lines

Automated progressive stamping dies and robotic welding cells maintain high daily output capacity to satisfy both large multi-year procurement programs and rapid allied surge replenishment requirements.

TRACEABILITY

Complete Lot Marking & Documentation

Containers are stamped with permanent manufacturer identification, drawing revision, and lot numbers. Every production batch is delivered with full Certificates of Conformance (CoC) and mill material test reports.

EXPORT SHIPMENT

Containerized International Logistics

Optimized container loading plans (20ft and 40ft High-Cube ocean containers) maximize freight density and minimize per-unit international freight costs for defense customers worldwide.
