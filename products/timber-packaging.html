Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging manufactures custom structural timber crates, heavy machinery skids, and arsenal storage boxes engineered strictly to US Military Specifications, including ASTM D6251, ASTM D6256, ASTM D6039, and international standard ISPM 15.

Designed to transport and protect heavy weapon components, guided missile assemblies, tank replacement engines, naval propulsion parts, and artillery barrels weighing up to 40,000 lbs. Equipped with automated heavy panel saws, CNC timber routing stations, and certified heat-treatment kilns to deliver rugged, dimensionally precise, and fully phytosanitary-compliant military wooden packaging.

Heavy-Duty Structural Integrity & International Compliance

Every wooden crate is constructed using high-grade structural timber (C24 Softwood or Group III/IV Hardwoods), reinforced with zinc-plated steel corner brackets, heavy lag-screw fasteners, and built-in 4-way lifting sling and forklift interfaces. Fully certified with official IPPC stamps for seamless worldwide tactical deployment.

Engineering Features & Heavy Defense Packaging Standards

Engineered to deliver exceptional rigidity, weather resistance, and multi-modal transit protection.

PHYTOSANITARY

IPPC ISPM 15 Certified Heat Treatment

Manufactured exclusively from heat-treated lumber subjected to core temperatures ≥ 56°C for ≥ 30 minutes in audited kilns. Stamped with official IPPC marks for unrestricted international ocean and air transport.

CNC CONTOURED CADLES

Precision Weapon Saddle Cradles

Multi-axis CNC routing carves custom curved cradles matching artillery barrel contours, missile motor casings, or vehicle transmissions, lined with heavy anti-chafing neoprene.

HEAVY HANDLING

4-Way Forklift & Crane Sling Access

Heavy skidded bases feature beveled 4-way forklift notches, heavy towing brackets, and dedicated crane rigging points designed to simplify field logistics and port handling.

DEPOT WEATHERPROOF

Barrier Lining & Chemical Preservatives

Interiors can be lined with MIL-PRF-131 Class 1 moisture-vapor barrier film and desiccant packs, while timber is treated with water-repellent preservatives for multi-year open depot storage.

Scalable Woodshop Capacity, DFM & Worldwide Deployment

Industrial timber converting facilities capable of processing over 2,000 plywood sheets and tens of thousands of board feet daily.

HEAVY SAWMILL CAPACITY

Automated Panel Saws & Assembly

High-precision automated beam saws, multi-axis timber routers, and pneumatic nailing bridges ensure rapid, accurate fabrication of both one-off custom crates and large production lots.

FLAT-PACK DELIVERY

Modular Knocked-Down Shipping

Crates can be delivered fully assembled or pre-fabricated in modular knocked-down panels with pre-drilled lag screw holes, saving significant freight volume to forward assembly depots.

STENCIL MARKING

MIL-STD-129 Identification & Placards

In-house computerized stenciling machines apply tactical markings, center of balance indicators, lifting sling locations, and hazard warning symbols per military standards.

EXPEDIENT SURGE SUPPLY

Urgent Defense Surge Capability

Extensive strategic inventory of ISPM 15 certified timber and hardware allows immediate fabrication and emergency deployment for urgent defense operations.
